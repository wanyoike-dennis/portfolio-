Who Am I?

I’m a passionate Software Engineer and technology enthusiast focused on building practical, modern, and user-friendly digital solutions. I enjoy turning ideas into functional applications and websites that solve real-world problems and create meaningful experiences for users.

My experience spans web development, Android development, software engineering, and database systems, with hands-on experience using technologies such as JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Kotlin, Java, HTML, CSS, SQL, and Tailwind CSS.

I’m particularly interested in creating clean and responsive interfaces, developing reliable applications, and continuously learning new technologies. I also enjoy exploring how technology can be used to improve businesses, automate processes, and make everyday tasks easier.

Beyond writing code, I value problem-solving, continuous learning, attention to detail, and building solutions that have a real impact. I’m always open to challenging projects and opportunities to grow as a developer while contributing to innovative technology solutions.
